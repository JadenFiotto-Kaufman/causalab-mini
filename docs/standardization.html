nnterp and mini: who knows what about a model's internals.

in progress

This page has not been written yet. It is one page of a set being filled in; the rest of the site is live.

What it will cover:

  • address.py is the only file that knows model internals — and most of what it knows it asks nnterp for.
  • A boundary is the name of an nnterp accessor; the module path, whether the place exists, which side, where it sits in the forward pass, and every width and head count are nnterp's.
  • What is still mini's: the per-head kind, the key axis, the seq axis, the width attribute name, read-only, and the four interiors with their rank.
  • No family axis was needed: equal addresses on tiny Llama and tiny GPT-2 for components whose module trees share no path.
  • The binding-suffix trap, and why mini addresses the call rather than an occurrence suffix. FINDINGS.md.
  • RenameConfig(addresses={…}): the table is a floor, not a fence.
